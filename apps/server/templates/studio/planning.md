# Planning

Adapted from Genex's director material (MIT), cut hard.

1. Look at the game first. Play the current build before writing anything.
2. Cut to the ask: one sentence for what the player does, one for why it matters.
3. Write PLAN.md: structure (screens/flow), rules and state, content list,
   asset list, milestones, and what done means. Numbers, not adjectives.
4. Order work in rungs: each rung changes what a player notices in the
   first minute. The last rung stays open for the next session.
5. Scope rule: deepen what was asked. No unasked systems.
6. A plan that fits the budget beats a complete one. Cut until it fits.
