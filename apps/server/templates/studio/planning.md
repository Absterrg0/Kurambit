# Planning

Adapted from Genex's director material (MIT), cut hard.

A game whose AGENTS.md has "The first turn" follows it: that turn writes no
plan or design document and goes straight to the build.

1. Look at the game first. Play the current build before writing anything.
2. Cut to the ask: one sentence for what the player does, one for why it matters.
3. Write PLAN.md: structure (screens/flow), rules and state apart from
   presentation, the asset list, what makes the core good (how it plays,
   feels and looks), and what done means for the scope asked. Numbers,
   not adjectives.
4. Scope rule: deepen what was asked. No unasked systems.
5. A plan that fits the budget beats a complete one. Cut until it fits.
