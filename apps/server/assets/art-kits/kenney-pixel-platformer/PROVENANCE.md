Only the packed tile atlas `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png` (360×162, 20×9
cells of 18×18 px), the packed character atlas
`Tilemap/tilemap-characters_packed.png` (216×72, 9×3 cells of 24×24 px) and the
archive's original `License.txt` are shipped. Both packed images have no margin
or spacing; the pack's `Tilesheet (*).txt` 1 px spacing applies to the unpacked
`Tilemap/tilemap*.png` variants, which are not shipped. The archive's individual
tile PNGs, Tiled maps, Construct project, previews and samples are not included.

The tile atlas includes terrain, platforms, props, items, clouds and digits. The
character atlas includes explorer characters, an astronaut, box and flying
enemies, a robot and a bat. The green explorer's two row-0 cells are used as a
two-frame step in the import render proof.
