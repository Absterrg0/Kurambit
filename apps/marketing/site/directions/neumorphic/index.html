Every round, a real process.

Kurambit runs the habits of a good team around your agent, round after round, until the budget ends.

  1. 1Brief

    One or two sentences. Genre, mood, art direction.

  2. 2Build

    A Godot project and Blender models, every source editable.

  3. 3Play

    A separate tester session plays it and reports what it saw.

  4. 4Judge

    Two builds, compared blind. Worse builds are reverted.

Your game in the middle. The agent at your side.

Play the real build in the app, watch every step the agent takes, and ask for changes in plain words. Everything stays on your machine.

Questions.

When can I use Kurambit?

We let people in small batches so every new studio gets a good first game. Join the waitlist and we'll email your invite.

What do I need?

Linux x86_64 today and one signed-in coding agent. Kurambit installs Godot and Blender for you.

What does it cost?

Kurambit is MIT-licensed and runs on the agent subscription you already have.

Do I own what it makes?

Yes. Every game is its own git repository with editable sources and .blend files.
