FAQ

Good questions.

When can I use Kurambit?

We're letting people in small batches so every new studio gets a good first game. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when your invite is ready.

What do I need?

A Linux x86_64 machine today and one signed-in coding agent. Kurambit installs Godot and Blender for you, and games export to Windows, Linux and the web.

What does it cost?

Kurambit is open source under the MIT license and runs on the agent subscription you already have. One model is enough for the whole loop.

Do I own what it makes?

Yes. Every game is its own git repository with editable sources, scripts and .blend files.
