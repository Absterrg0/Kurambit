01Studio

Describe a game in a sentence. Your coding agent builds it, a separate tester plays every build, and a blind judge keeps only what's better.

  • StageThe real build, playable in the middle of the app.
  • AgentEvery step it takes, in plain view on the right.

03Process

  1. 1BriefOne or two sentences. Genre, mood, art direction.
  2. 2BuildGodot project and Blender models. Every source editable.
  3. 3PlayA separate tester session plays it and reports what it saw.
  4. 4JudgeTwo builds, compared blind. Worse builds are reverted.

04FAQ

When can I use it?
We let people in small batches. Join the waitlist and we'll email your invite.
What do I need?
Linux x86_64 today, and one signed-in coding agent: Codex, Claude Code, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenCode or Antigravity.
What does it cost?
Nothing. Kurambit is MIT-licensed and runs on the agent subscription you already have.
Who owns the games?
You. Each game is its own git repository with editable sources and .blend files.
