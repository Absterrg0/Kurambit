The stack

Four layers, every round.

Anyone can ask a model for a game. Kurambit wraps it in the habits of a real team, and keeps going until the budget ends.

  1. Brief

    A sentence or two. Genre, mood, art direction.

  2. Build

    Your agent writes the Godot project and models assets in Blender.

  3. Play

    A separate tester session plays the build and reports what it saw.

  4. Judge

    Two builds compared blind. Ties and worse builds are reverted.

The studio

Your game in the middle. The agent at your side.

Play the real build inside the app, watch every step the agent takes, and ask for changes in plain words. Everything stays on your machine.

FAQ

Questions.

When can I use it?

We let people in small batches. Join the waitlist and we'll email your invite.

What do I need?

Linux x86_64 today and one signed-in coding agent. Kurambit installs Godot and Blender for you.

What does it cost?

Kurambit is MIT-licensed and runs on the agent subscription you already have.

Who owns the games?

You. Each game is its own git repository with editable sources and .blend files.
