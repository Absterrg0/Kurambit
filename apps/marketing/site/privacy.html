Privacy

Your details stay with us.

This note covers the waitlist on kurambit.com. The Kurambit app itself runs on your own machine and keeps its data there.

What we collect

Your email address, and if you choose to share them, your name and what describes you best. We also keep the time you signed up and which form you used.

To stop abuse we keep a one-way, keyed hash of your IP address for a short rate-limit window. We never store the address itself, and the hash cannot be turned back into it.

Why

Only to contact you about Kurambit: your invite, and the occasional launch update. We do not sell, rent or share your details, and we do not add you to anyone else's list.

Where it lives

In a private Postgres database that only the Kurambit server can reach, over encrypted connections. The list is never published, and no page on this site can read it.

Leaving the list

Reply to any email from us, or write to us through GitHub, and we will delete your details.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no third-party trackers or analytics scripts.
