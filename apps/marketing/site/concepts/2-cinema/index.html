The making of

Every round, the same four acts. A worse cut never reaches the screen.

  1. Act I

    The brief

    A sentence about the game you want. Kurambit sets up a real project and briefs your agent.

  2. Act II

    The build

    Scenes and scripts in Godot, models in Blender, every source kept editable in its own git repository.

  3. Act III

    The screening

    A separate tester session plays the build and writes down what it actually saw.

  4. Act IV

    The verdict

    Two cuts, judged blind. Only the better one is kept, and the next round begins.
