Kurambit · site directions

Five ways Kurambit could introduce itself.

Each one is a complete page with the waitlist built in. Open them, scroll, play with them, then pick one to grow.

  1. 01

    Arcade

    The site is a game. A CRT title screen, an attract-mode cabinet cycling real Kurambit games, character select for your agent, and the waitlist as a high-score entry.

  2. 02

    Cinema

    A film studio's homepage. Full-bleed key art, a "Now showing" rail of real games with the exact sentence each came from, the making-of in four acts, and a credits roll.

  3. 03

    Ink

    An exhibition catalogue. Washi paper, the brushed 狐, a hanko seal, games hung as scrolls, and a guestbook to sign. Quiet, crafted, unmistakable.

  4. 04

    Handheld

    Playful hardware. A console built in code that actually plays the games, A and B to switch, a spec sheet, and cartridges. Joyful and very ownable.

  5. 05

    Manifesto

    A Swiss poster with an opinion. Giant condensed type, a halftone fox, six principles, a ticker, and footage that turns to colour when you look at it.
