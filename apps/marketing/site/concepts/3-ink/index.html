The fox's workshop · 狐の工房

Games, made by an agent that plays them.

Kurambit is a quiet studio for your coding agent. Give it one line about a game. It builds in Godot and Blender, plays every build, and lets a blind judge decide what stays.

I — IV

Four strokes,
every round.

  1. I

    The brief

    One line is enough. “A small game about a lighthouse keeper guiding ships through fog.”

  2. II

    The making

    Your agent writes the engine project and models in Blender, keeping every source.

  3. III

    The playing

    A separate tester sits with each build and notes what it truly saw.

  4. IV

    The judging

    Two builds, unsigned. Only the better one is kept.
