Hollow Reach · round 4 to 5

Every build gets played by someone who didn't build it.

A separate tester session plays each build and writes down what actually happened. The builder fixes it. A judge compares the two builds blind. This is one real round.

  1. Tester Play 2 · 17 acts

    I held W for a full three seconds and the pilgrim didn't move a pixel. No bump sound, no hint, just a static frame while I held keys that did nothing. A block I can see is a puzzle; a block I can't feel is just dead input.

  2. Builder Round 5 · 19 files
    • +Centre magnet on the ramp base
    • +Pillar bump with “Blocked — centre for the ramp”
    • +Under-terrace clamp: “The terrace looms — take the ramp”
    • +Warden vision cones drawn on the ground
    • −Silent walk under the terrace
  3. Judge Blind · A vs B
    Build A Build B · kept

    Off-centre pushes into the ramp mouth now climb or get visibly redirected instead of walking silently under the terrace. Worse builds are reverted, never merged. Ties too.

How it works

A studio's habits, every round, until the budget ends.

  1. 1 Brief

    One or two sentences. Genre, mood, art direction. Kurambit turns it into a plan.

  2. 2 Build

    Your agent writes the Godot project and models assets in Blender. Every source stays editable.

  3. 3 Play

    A separate tester session plays the build and reports what it saw, not what it was told.

  4. 4 Judge

    Two builds, compared blind. A worse build is reverted. The round starts again.

Questions

Good to know.

When can I use it?
We let people in small batches so every new studio gets a good first game. Join the waitlist and we'll email your invite.
What do I need?
A Linux x86_64 machine today and one signed-in coding agent. Kurambit installs Godot and Blender for you. Games export to Windows, Linux and the web.
What does it cost?
Kurambit is open source under the MIT license. It runs on the agent subscription you already have, and one model is enough for the whole loop.
Who owns the games?
You do. Every game is its own git repository with editable scenes, scripts and .blend files. Take it anywhere.
